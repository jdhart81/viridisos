# Support

Use public issues for reproducible bugs, documentation gaps, module proposals,
and non-sensitive questions. Include the version, operating system, exact
command, expected behavior, and sanitized output.

Use the private security route for vulnerabilities, personal data, private
keys, or confidential conduct reports. Research corrections should identify
the exact Zenodo DOI and theorem. Commercial support, production trust-root
access, mark licensing, deployment, and settlement are outside community
support and require a separate agreement.
