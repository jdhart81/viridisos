# Trademark and certification-mark boundary

The Apache-2.0 software license and CC-BY-4.0 documentation license do not grant
permission to claim endorsement, operate the Viridis production trust root, or
issue an authoritative certificate using the "Certified by ViridisOS" mark.

Forks may accurately state that they use or are derived from the ViridisOS
reference runtime. They must label self-issued certificates as self-rooted,
must not imitate official marks, and must not imply that Viridis reviewed or
approved an output. Formal mark-use and cross-certification terms require a
separate written agreement.
